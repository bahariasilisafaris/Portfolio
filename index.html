Questions

Questions, answered plainly.

Still wondering something? Ask a person — I reply within a day.

What exactly is included?

The live site as you see it, the full source code in a Git repository, the build and run notes, and a set-up guide for the domain and hosting. If you want changes before handover — your logo, your copy, your colours — say so and we’ll price them in.

How do domain and hosting transfer?

The sites currently live on my Vercel accounts. Transfer means inviting your account (or your engineer’s) and pointing your domain at it — usually under an hour of work. I stay on WhatsApp until it’s done and the site is loading on your name.

Do I get the source code?

Yes — all of it, with commit history. No lock-in, no hidden files kept back. Once it’s handed over, your engineer can change anything without asking me.

Is handover support included?

Yes. A walkthrough call for you or your engineer, written notes, and WhatsApp follow-up questions for two weeks after handover. If your engineer needs more time, we can agree extra at an hourly rate.

How does payment work?

We agree a price first, in writing on WhatsApp. Half up front, half when you’re happy and the transfer is done. M-Pesa or bank transfer, receipt every time. No subscription, no monthly fee owed to me.

Can you customise a site for my business?

That’s the usual route. New name, new colours, your services and prices, your photos, your booking flow — I can reshape any of the three to fit your business before handover, or build something new from scratch. Ask and I’ll quote.
